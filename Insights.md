Key Business Insights – Northwind SQL Analysis



\## 1. Order Distribution by Country

The analysis shows that orders are distributed across multiple countries, with a few countries contributing the majority of total orders. This indicates strong demand in specific regions and highlights opportunities for focused marketing and regional expansion.



\## 2. Product Pricing Analysis

High-priced products represent premium offerings, while lower-priced products may drive higher sales volume. Understanding this pricing mix helps balance revenue growth and customer demand.



\## 3. Inventory Insights

Several products have low stock levels, which may lead to stockouts if demand increases. These products should be prioritized for inventory replenishment to avoid potential revenue loss.



\## 4. Customer Order Behavior

A small group of customers places a higher number of orders compared to others, indicating the presence of high-value customers. These customers can be targeted with loyalty programs and personalized offers.



\## 5. Monthly Order Trend

Order volume shows a consistent pattern over time, which helps identify seasonality and demand trends. This insight can be used for better planning of inventory and staffing.
